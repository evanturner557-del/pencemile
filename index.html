Questions

Common questions

Which rates does it use?

HMRC approved mileage rates for cars and vans: 45p for the first 10,000 business miles, 25p after. Check GOV.UK in case rates change.

Does it cover motorbikes and bicycles?

Not yet. They have different rates.

Is commuting included?

No. Ordinary commuting isn't business mileage.
